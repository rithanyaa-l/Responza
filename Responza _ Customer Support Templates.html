What is this tool?
Responza keeps ready-made replies for your support team in one place. New joinees can quickly find the right answer by module, keyword or title, copy it and reply to the customer.
Videos and documents
Open Videos or Documents from the menu, the hero buttons or the Learning hub. Upload files, search them by name or module, and link related videos and documents together. Files stay in this browser.
Header menu
The menu at the top stays in view while you scroll. Use Find a reply, Add template and Saved templates to jump to that part of the page, and Help to open this guide. On a phone, tap ☰ to open the menu.
Create a template
  1. Go to Add new template at the top of the page.
  2. Fill the fields marked with a red *: Module name, Severity, Template type, Keywords and both description boxes, Problem? No Problem! (the reply sent to the customer) and Problem? Let's Debug! (internal debugging steps for the tech team, never sent to the customer). The title is optional.
  3. Type a keyword and press Enter to add it as a tag. Click × on a tag to remove it.
  4. Write the customer reply in the green No Problem box and the tech-team debugging steps in the amber Debug box. The copy buttons on cards and in Find a reply copy only the customer reply; Share sends both parts. You can type placeholders such as {customer}, {module}, {ticket} and {agent} and replace them before sending.
  5. Click Save template. A green message confirms it is saved.

Severity is Low, Medium, High or Critical and shows as a colour on the card. Type is Text (chat or email replies) or Voice (phone call scripts).

Find the right template
  • Use the Module name, Keyword and Template title filters. They work together.
  • Starred only shows your favourites.
  • The pills next to "Saved templates" switch between All, Text and Voice templates.
  • The ✕ Clear button appears only when a filter is applied. Click it to reset everything.
Work with a template card
  • ☆ Star marks a favourite, and starred templates move to the top.
  • 📋 Copy icon copies the template text.
  • 🗑 Delete icon asks for confirmation before deleting.
  • Read more opens the full text of every card in that row at once, and Show less closes the row again.
  • 🔗 Share offers Copy text, Email, WhatsApp and other apps.
  • ✏️ Edit opens a small popup. Change the fields and click Save changes.

Long module names are shortened with …. Hover over one to see the full name.

Match a customer message or screenshot
  • Under an AI reply, tap 👍 or 👎 to tell us if it helped. Counts are kept per template so weak templates are easy to spot.
  • If AI is switched on for your team, it works automatically: after you stop typing (or add a screenshot) the AI reads the message and screenshot, picks the best template and writes a reply for you to review.
  • Paste what the customer wrote into the Find a reply from the customer's message box. A Suggested reply appears, filled in from the best matching template. Edit it if needed, then click the copy to clipboard icon at the top right of the Suggested reply box.
  • Got a screenshot? Click Upload screenshot, paste it with Ctrl+V, or drag it into the box. The page reads the text in the image (internet needed), finds a matching template and adds a thank-you line for the screenshot. Typing a few words such as "this is the issue" or the module name makes the match better.
  • Other matching templates are listed below. Click Use this reply to switch to one.
  • If nothing matches, a general reply is suggested so you always have something to send.
Sort, coverage and bulk actions
  • Sort by offers Newest, A–Z, Severity or Most used. Starred templates always stay on top.
  • Module coverage lists each module with its Low, Medium, High and Critical counts and flags modules that have no High or Critical template. Click a row to filter by that module.
  • Tick the box on cards (or Select all) to Share, Export (JSON or CSV) or Delete several templates at once.
Owner and last updated
Each card shows when it was last updated and how many times it was copied. The person who created or last updated it is shown on the right side of the card. The page asks for your name the first time you save. You can change it from the footer.
Import templates from a file

Click the upload icon next to "Saved templates" and choose a .json or .csv file. Imported templates are added to the top of your list.

JSON example

[{"title":"Login help","keywords":["login","password"],"module":"Accounts","severity":"High","type":"text","no_problem":"Hi {customer}, please reset your password.","debug":"Please share a screenshot of the error and your user ID."}]

CSV: the first row holds column names: title, keywords, module, severity, type, no_problem, debug (an older body column also works). Separate several keywords with ; or |. Only a description is needed to import: no_problem and/or debug, or body. Templates missing either part can be completed later with Edit. Add any missing module, severity or keywords later with Edit.

My profile and log out
  • Click My profile in the top menu to see your details and how many templates you created and updated.
  • Change your name, email, role or team and click Save profile.
  • Log out asks for confirmation, then clears your name and profile from this browser. Your templates stay saved. Sign in again with your name to continue.
Tips for new joinees
  • Start with the module filter, then narrow down by keyword.
  • Check the severity colour on the card: green is Low, amber is Medium, red is High and dark red is Critical.
  • Always read the reply and replace any placeholders before sending it to the customer.
  • Star the templates you use every day so they stay at the top.
Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Only in this browser. Other people cannot see it, and clearing your browser data removes it. Use Share to send a template to a teammate.

Why can't I save a template? A required field (red *) is empty. The message at the top tells you which one.

Can I change a template later? Yes. Use Edit on the card, or the trash icon to delete it.
